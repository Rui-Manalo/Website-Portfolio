About

I like data that argues with you.

I'm less interested in the chart at the end and more in whether the numbers feeding it can be trusted. That's what pulled me toward analytics engineering — writing the SQL and dbt models that turn messy raw tables into something clean, tested, and safe for someone else to build a decision on.

I'm still early in this track, learning dbt, orchestration with Airflow, and cloud warehousing hands-on through my own projects. I care more about whether a pipeline is reliable than whether it's fancy — it should run on schedule, fail loudly when something's wrong, and be easy for the next person to understand.
