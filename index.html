About

I like data that argues with you.

Most of my work starts with a question a stakeholder can't quite answer on their own — "why did retention drop in March," "which channel actually pays for itself." I trace it back through the data, build the model or dashboard that answers it, and make sure the answer survives someone poking at it.

Before analytics I worked in operations, which is probably why I care more about whether a number is right than whether the chart is pretty. (Though I still try for both.)
